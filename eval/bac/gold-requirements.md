## Source Conflicts Resolved

1. **Governing documents.** The RFP body + Response Sheet (Excel) are binding. Turnwise Product Document 1 and UTAM Solution Architecture (WAISL Draft v1) are vendor collateral/sales context and are NOT requirement sources. Any requirement appearing only in collateral is excluded from this gold set.

2. **UTAM geography/regulatory mismatch.** The UTAM draft references "AIA" (Athens International Airport), "Athens Airport", "Brisbaine Airport" (misspelling), "AWS EU Regions", "GDPR", "NIS2", and "Eurocontrol NM" messaging. These conflict with the BAC Brisbane Airport context and Australian regulatory frame. **Resolution:** Disregarded. BAC is Brisbane Airport Corporation (BNE), Australia; the RFP/Response Sheet governs. No EU/GDPR/NIS2/Eurocontrol-NM obligation is imported into the gold set. Data sovereignty is addressed only via ISRA row 19 (Intellectual Property / data sovereignty) — a supplier response is required, but no specific region is mandated by the RFP.

3. **Functional requirement scope in collateral.** UTAM lists "FR Ref: FR01–FR09, FR13–FR19, FR24–FR59" as covered, implying some FRs are out of scope. **Resolution:** The Response Sheet Tab F is authoritative — all FR01–FR73 are in scope and must receive a conformance response; MoSCoW classification in the sheet determines mandatory vs optional.

4. **Severity/response SLAs.** NF19/NF20 specify Sev1/2/3 response and resolution times. These are restated in the Procedural (P-29…P-35) and Numeric (N-9…N-15) sections for scoring but are not duplicated as separate requirements; they originate in SUB-NF-19 / SUB-NF-20.

5. **PMR-10 classification.** The Response Sheet marks PMR-10 (Defects Liability & System Maintenance, 6-month defects liability, maintenance agreement) as "Should have" in the MoSCoW column. **Resolution:** Classified as Optional, despite the strongly worded requirement text. Drafts that deliver it score above-baseline; drafts that omit it are not penalised for a mandatory miss.

6. **RFP §4.3 "Contract Extensions" vs. §5.2.** §5.2 defers term to §4.3 — no conflict; §4.3 governs (3 years initial + 2×1-year extensions).

7. **Pricing schedule mismatch with contract term.** Pricing template asks for a 5-year cost plan (Year 1–5) while the contract initial term is 3 years + 2×1-year extensions (= 5 years total potential). **Resolution:** No conflict — the 5-year pricing horizon aligns with the maximum possible term. Captured as N-25.

8. **Insurance amounts.** RFP §4.4 sets Workers Compensation (per Act), Public Liability ≥ $20m, Professional Indemnity $10m, Cyber Security $10m. The Response Sheet Supplier Information §4 asks the supplier to list Insurer/Policy/Level/Expiry but labels rows "Indemnity Insurance / Public Liability / Other Insurance" (with typos and a duplicated section mislabelled "Certification" §5). **Resolution:** RFP §4.4 amounts govern (N-1 to N-5); the Response Sheet is the template for evidence. Certificate of Currencies must be appended (C-10).

9. **"Schedule 6 / Tab F" vs. response-sheet tab labels.** The RFP §8 table mislabels Schedule content in places (e.g. Schedule 5 Tab E described as "Pricing" but row text says "recent relevant experience for key personnel"; Schedule 3 Tab C described as "Relevant Experience" but row text says "sub-contractors"). **Resolution:** The actual Response Sheet tabs (Supplier Information, Social Procurement, Relevant Experience, Methodology, Pricing, Functional Requirements, NF Requirements, PM Requirements, ISRA) govern content; the RFP §8 schedule list is treated as indicative only.

## Notes for Scorers

- **Mandatory denominator = 269.** A draft's compliance score = (count of mandatory requirements satisfied) / 269.
- **Optional requirements (6 total: S-10, SUB-F-06, SUB-F-12, SUB-F-39, SUB-F-48, SUB-PMR-20)** are not in the denominator but may be tracked separately as "value-add".
- **"Tentative TBC" dates** (contract commencement 7 Sep 2026, Go-Live 11 Dec 2026) are treated as binding targets for response purposes; a draft that commits to them satisfies the requirement.
- A conformance response of "Partial" against a Must-Have FR/NF/PMR row counts as non-compliant for mandatory scoring unless the draft's detail explicitly closes the gap.
- ISRA rows require both a Supplier Response and a Residual Risk rating; missing either is a miss.
