## KEY NUMERIC SUMMARIES

### Licensing & Kiosk Coverage
- **Initial Deployment:** 50 fixed + 10 roaming kiosks = 60 total kiosks
- **Phase Implementation:** 60 fixed + 15 roaming kiosks = 75 total kiosks
- **Terminal Coverage:** 3 terminals (T1, T2, T3)
- **License Model:** Perpetual, unlimited usage by DIAL
- **Avatar Options:** Minimum 5 different attire configurations
- **Language Support:** 15 languages minimum (6 Indian + 9 International)

### Availability & Performance
- **Uptime SLA:** 99.5% availability
- **MTTR:** ≤ 4 hours mean time to repair
- **Alert Deployment:** ≤ 5 minutes for urgent announcements
- **Offline Capability:** Cached flight/navigation data available during outages

### Timeline & Milestones (Days from PO Issuance - T0)
1. Day 7: Advance bank guarantee (10% payment)
2. Day 15: FRS & SRS approval (10% payment)
3. Day 60: Pilot deployment on 4-6 kiosks (10% payment)
4. Day 75: VAPT completion (10% payment)
5. Day 90: Full deployment (35% payment)
6. Day 100: UAT completion (15% payment)
7. Day 90 (from DLP start): DLP completion (10% payment)

### Change Request Entitlements
- **Pre-Go-Live:** 10 CRs total (3 Major + 7 Minor max)
- **DLP Period:** 10 additional CRs (3 Major + 7 Minor max)
- **Minor CR Threshold:** ≤ 3 person-days effort
- **Major CR Threshold:** > 3 and ≤ 10 person-days
- **CR Response Time:** Classification within 3 business days

### Hardware Specifications
- **Display:** 43-inch multi-touch (portrait)
- **Height:** ~8 feet floor-mounted
- **RAM:** 16 GB minimum
- **Processor:** Intel-based
- **Architecture:** Fanless industrial design for 24/7 operation

### Security & Compliance
- **Password Minimum:** 8 characters + 3 of 4 character types
- **Credential Revalidation:** ≤ 6 months non-use threshold
- **Termination Deactivation:** ≤ 2 business days
- **VAPT Frequency:** Minimum 1 annually
- **Patch Management:** ≤ 90 days maximum for all patches
- **Data Breach Notification:** ≤ 2 hours of awareness
- **PII Notification:** ≤ 5 calendar days

### Financial Penalties
- **Delay Penalty:** 5% per week (capped at 30%)
- **Avatar/Feature Non-Compliance:** INR 10,000/day
- **Design/Content Non-Compliance:** INR 20,000/occurrence (3+ times)
- **Kiosk Downtime:** INR 10K (1st) → INR 25K (2nd) → INR 50K (3+)
- **Data Breach:** INR 1,00,000+ per incident
- **VAPT Remediation:** INR 25,000/day (Critical/High) | INR 10,000/day (Medium/Low)
- **Annual Penalty Cap:** 30% of annual contract value

### Defect Severity Levels (DLP Exit Criteria)
- **Severity 1/Critical:** Complete outage, widespread unavailability, security incident
- **Severity 2/High:** Major degradation affecting multiple kiosks
- **Severity 3/Medium:** Partial degradation with workaround available
- **Severity 4/Low:** Cosmetic/minor issues with negligible operational impact
