## 🎯 KEY LICENSING FRAMEWORK SUMMARY

### AIOP Platform (Operational Digital Twin)
- **IP Ownership:** WAISL (perpetual, exclusive)
- **DIAL's Rights:** Perpetual, royalty-free licence for IGI Airport use only
- **Restrictions:** No unbundling, no resale, no competing platform use
- **Scope:** Deployment, operation, maintenance for duration of project and in perpetuity thereafter

### Geospatial Digital Twin
- **IP Ownership:** GEOKNO (+ ESRI ArcGIS integration)
- **DIAL's Rights:** Perpetual, royalty-free licence; expandable to other DIAL properties with escalation
- **Note:** DIAL assumes ESRI ArcGIS renewal responsibility post-contract

### AI Models & Training Data
- **IP Ownership:** DIAL (absolute, perpetual)
- **DIAL's Rights:** Use, retrain, export, migrate without restriction
- **Bidder Restriction:** WAISL cannot use DIAL data for external AI model training or competitive intelligence

### Custom Code & Integrations
- **IP Ownership:** WAISL (with perpetual licence to DIAL)
- **DIAL's Rights:** Modify, maintain, integrate; no resale without consent
- **Scope:** System connectors, domain rules engines, UI customisations, data mappings

### Third-Party Components
- **Scope:** ESRI ArcGIS, Unity/Unreal Engine, cloud services, open-source libraries
- **DIAL's Rights:** Included in AIOP licence; no additional fees
- **Compliance:** Full SBOM provided (Annexure K.6); no GPL copyleft in AIOP core

### Source Code Escrow
- **Trigger Events:** WAISL bankruptcy, acquisition by competitor, support failure (30+ days)
- **Deliverables:** AIOP source code, Geo DT algorithms, custom code, technical documentation
- **Post-Release:** DIAL can maintain, operate, migrate without WAISL approval
- **Cost:** WAISL funds initial deposit; DIAL reimburses 50% of renewal fees post-contract (negotiable)

## 🎓 KEY CONCEPTS EMBEDDED IN SECTION 5.5a

1. **Perpetual Licence Clarity:** DIAL's usage rights persist beyond Year 5 (contract + 5-year O&M) and into perpetuity with no additional fees. Addresses DIAL's concern about vendor lock-in and long-term operational cost certainty.

2. **Exclusive Deployment:** AIOP licence applies exclusively to IGI Airport; WAISL cannot license competing platforms for the same scope (e.g., ArcGIS, Autodesk) during contract term + 5-year O&M. Protects DIAL's competitive positioning.

3. **AI Model Ownership:** DIAL owns all trained models, weights, and training data in perpetuity. WAISL cannot use DIAL data for external model training or competitive intelligence. Aligns with BRD 3.5.5 and DIAL's data-sovereignty policy.

4. **Escrow & Business Continuity:** Formal escrow arrangement ensures DIAL can access source code if WAISL becomes unable to provide support. Mitigates operational risk post-contract expiry.

5. **No GPL Copyleft:** AIOP core is not subject to GPL copyleft obligations, protecting DIAL from forced open-sourcing of configurations or integrations. Open-source components documented in SBOM.

6. **Post-Contract Support:** DIAL can engage WAISL (or competitors) for maintenance on time-and-materials basis post-contract. Enables vendor flexibility and competitive bidding for ongoing support.

7. **Third-Party Renewal Responsibility:** ESRI ArcGIS (Geo DT foundation) renewal transitions to DIAL post-contract, with clear budget visibility. Avoids surprise renewal costs.
